# Privacy Policy: SuiteScript Version Guard

Last updated: 20 October 2026

SuiteScript Version Guard is a Chrome extension that saves versions of SuiteScript files you choose to track in your own NetSuite account, and shows you what changed between them.

## What data the extension handles

- **Script files you choose to track.** When you track a NetSuite file, the extension reads that file from your NetSuite account, using the NetSuite session you are already signed in to. The file content, the file ID, the file name and the NetSuite account URL are stored.
- **Settings** (for example, how often to check for changes).

## Where the data is stored

All data is stored **only in your own browser** (IndexedDB and Chrome extension storage on your computer). The extension has no server, no account system and no database of its own.

## What we do not do

- We **do not collect, receive, transmit or sell** your data. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.
- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no remote code.
- The extension does not read your NetSuite password or cookies. It makes ordinary requests to your NetSuite domain, which your browser authenticates as it normally does.
- The extension only accesses `https://*.app.netsuite.com/` pages.

## Your control

- You decide which files are tracked. Stop tracking a file to delete its saved versions.
- You can export all saved data to a file (Export backup) and import it again.
- Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data from your browser.

## Your responsibility

Saved script files are copies of code from your NetSuite account and are kept on your computer. Make sure you are allowed to keep copies of that code under your employer's or client's policies.
